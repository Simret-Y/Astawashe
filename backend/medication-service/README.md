# AstaWashe — Medication, Reminder & Adherence Service

## Overview

The Medication, Reminder & Adherence Service is a core backend module of
AstaWashe, an AI-powered, audio-first medication reminder and health
assistance platform.

This module is responsible for managing medications and prescriptions,
creating medication schedules, generating reminders, tracking medication
adherence, and notifying guardians when medication doses are missed.

---

## Responsibilities

This service is responsible for:

- Medication management
- Prescription management
- Medication scheduling
- Reminder generation and tracking
- Medication adherence tracking
- Missed-dose detection
- Guardian alert generation
- Notification integration
- SMS, push, and voice reminder support
